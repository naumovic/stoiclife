🌙 Evening Review

The Wins: What did you do well today? When did you successfully practice a virtue? Were you able to maintain your focus on being present?

The Lessons: Where did you stumble, lose your temper, or find yourself bothered by something outside your control?

The Adjustment: Knowing what you know now, what will you do differently tomorrow to improve?

🧠 The Mogi Brain Check

The Nagomi Factor: Was there a "small win" today that brought you a sense of harmony or simple joy during your time with your family?

Amygdala vs. Prefrontal Cortex: Did you have a moment where your "fight or flight" response kicked in today, and were you able to use logic to calm yourself down?
